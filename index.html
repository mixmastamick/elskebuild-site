Ballast: Capacity Planner

A task app that plans your day around how much you actually have to give.

Most to-do apps assume every day is the same. Ballast starts each morning with a quick check-in on your capacity, then builds a short list that fits. Tasks you skip aren't failures. They return to the pool, and over time Ballast shows you the patterns.

  • Add tasks by typing or by voice, with an effort level and optional reminder
  • A daily list sized to your capacity
  • Projects, labels, and a private daily reflection

Ballast is a $1.99 per month subscription with a 3-day free trial, for iPhone and iPad.
